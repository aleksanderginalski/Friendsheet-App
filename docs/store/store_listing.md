### Full Description
```
Friendsheet helps you stay on top of your social life by logging who you meet, what you do together, and how meaningful each interaction is.

Whether you're the type who loses track of old friends or just wants to be more intentional about relationships — Friendsheet gives you the structure to do it.

KEY FEATURES

▸ Log meetings — name, date, participants, and a Fibonacci-scale importance weight
▸ Manage friends — searchable list with per-person meeting history
▸ Activity categories — hierarchical activity tree (global + your own private categories)
▸ Statistics — see your most frequent contacts, activity breakdown, and "haven't seen in a while" alerts
▸ Data export — download your full history as JSON at any time
▸ Google Calendar import — turn past calendar events into meetings with one tap
▸ Offline-first — works without internet, syncs when back online

BUILT WITH

Flutter · Firebase (Auth + Firestore) · Google Sign-In · Provider · Freezed

Clean architecture with repository pattern, dependency injection, and full test coverage. Open source portfolio project.

PRIVACY

Your data lives in your own Firestore space — no other user can access it. Google Sign-In is the only authentication method. We don't sell or share your data.

Read our full Privacy Policy at the link below.
```
